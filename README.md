# Pixel Invaders

A retro space-shooter built by **LaunchTower**. Rows of aliens march down the screen in waves — move your ship, fire, and clear the grid before they reach you. Survive each wave to push into the next, and keep your **Best** score climbing.

## Try it

▶️ [Play Pixel Invaders in your browser](https://pennypenguinapp-beep.github.io/launchtower-pixel-invaders/)

No install, no account, no upload — it runs 100% in your browser.

## Features

- Classic **wave-based** alien shooter with a live **Score**, **Wave**, and **Lives (3)** HUD
- Keyboard controls: **← →** or **A / D** to move, **SPACE** to fire, **P** to pause
- Mobile-friendly on-screen controls: hold **◀ / ▶** to steer, tap **FIRE** to shoot
- **Best** score tracking so you can chase your high score
- Lightweight single-file HTML/JS — loads instantly, no dependencies

## How it works

The whole game is a single `index.html` file that runs entirely in your browser using HTML5 Canvas and vanilla JavaScript. Your input, score, and lives all live on your device — nothing is uploaded, tracked, or sent anywhere.
